Overview of Our IP Protection Services

At AuraProtect, we provide a suite of tools designed to safeguard your cultural assets in the digital age. Our services include:

  • AI-Powered IP Monitoring: Continuous automated scanning across the web to detect unauthorized use of your registered assets. We alert you to potential infringements.
  • Digital Watermarking: Subtly embed imperceptible watermarks into your digital assets (images, audio, video) to prove ownership and deter theft.
  • Legal Assistance Framework: While we don't offer direct legal services, we provide resources, template documents, and connections to legal professionals specializing in IP law in Nigeria. (Future Enhancement)
  • Asset Registration: A secure platform to register your creative works, creating a timestamped record of your ownership.

How It Works: Protecting Your Assets

Getting started with AuraProtect's IP protection is simple:

  1. Register as a Creator: Sign up on our platform and complete your creator profile.
  2. Upload Your Asset: Submit your digital asset (artwork, music file, manuscript, etc.) through your creator dashboard.
  3. Provide Asset Details: Add relevant information about your work, including title, description, and creation date.
  4. Choose Protection Tier: Select a pricing plan that suits your needs (details below). Basic registration may be free, with advanced features like AI monitoring available in premium tiers.
  5. Automated Protection: Once submitted and approved (if applicable), your asset can be digitally watermarked and entered into our AI monitoring system (based on your subscription).
  6. Receive Alerts: Get notified through your dashboard of any potential infringements detected by our AI.
